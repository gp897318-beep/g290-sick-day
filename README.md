A make.fun entry for [The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-the-funniest-sick-day-excuse-37423a36): one line, under 200 characters, that gets you out of work and gets a laugh — judged pairwise by a comedy-editor judge on whether a boss might half believe it, whether the idea is fresh, and whether the laugh lands before the last word.

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` after validating it against the gate (1–200 characters, single line, not only whitespace):

> I sneezed so hard my back filed a formal complaint, so I can't stand up straight enough to look employed today.

Why this line: a sneeze throwing your back out is something a boss can half believe — everyone knows someone it happened to. The back "filing a formal complaint" is office bureaucracy inside the body, a fresh turn rather than a worn fever or food-poisoning excuse. And the funniest words — "look employed" — land last, in a line that reads like a real text message.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the excuse as a single line to `/output/entry.txt`.
